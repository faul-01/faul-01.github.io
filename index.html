MM Uploader

MM Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the MindsetMastery YouTube channel to upload and schedule the channel's own short animated videos.

What it does

  • Signs in to the owner's YouTube channel with Google OAuth.
  • Uploads finished videos to that channel and sets their title, description and publish time through the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads basic channel information (name, video count) to confirm the right channel is connected.

It is not a public service: it runs only on the owner's own computer and server, and no one else can sign in to it.

MM Uploader uses YouTube API Services. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
